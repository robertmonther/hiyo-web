Child safety standards

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Test version. These rules are in force for Hiyo's test. They will be checked and completed before the public launch; if anything changes, the app asks you to read and accept them again.

Hiyo has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This applies to everyone, everywhere in Hiyo: posts, reels, comments, profiles, groups, messages and Bub.

What is forbidden

  • Any image, video, text or AI-made content that sexualises a person under 18.
  • Grooming: contacting or befriending a minor with a sexual aim; asking a minor for intimate images.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, and any other sexual exploitation of children.
  • Sharing, asking for or pointing to such material anywhere else.

How Hiyo protects young people

  • People must be at least 13 to join and give their birth date. Under 18, Hiyo is safer: no 18+ posts or groups, and chats only with friends, so strangers can't message them.
  • 18+ content must carry a label and stays hidden from minors.
  • Every post, reel, profile, group and message has Report, with a reason for "sexual content with someone under 18". Anyone can block anyone.

What we do when we learn of CSAE

  • We remove the content at once and close the accounts involved.
  • We keep what the law requires as evidence and report it to the authorities: in the Netherlands the police and the Expertisebureau Online Kindermisbruik (Offlimits), and the US National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where it applies.
  • We follow the child safety laws of the Netherlands and the European Union.

Contact for child safety

Hiyo's child safety contact is Robert Monther. Use Report in the app; the email address for child safety questions is added here before the public launch. If a child is in danger right now, call the police (112 in Europe).
