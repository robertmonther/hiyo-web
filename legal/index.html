Hiyo's rules

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Test version. These rules are in force for Hiyo's test. They will be checked and completed before the public launch; if anything changes, the app asks you to read and accept them again.

Hiyo is a social app for people who are tired of wars: a place where ordinary people, not the giants, meet, talk, create, support each other and publish. These pages say how we live together here and what happens with your data.

  • Constitution: what Hiyo stands for, in eight short articles.
  • Terms of use: what you may post, what is not allowed, how reports and blocking work.
  • Privacy policy: what Hiyo stores, why, who helps us run it, and your rights.
  • Child safety standards: zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.
  • Delete your account: how to delete your account and everything in it.

Everyone accepts these rules in the app before posting. The short version: no war, a free life, respect, people only (no media outlets, companies, parties or states), and your data belongs to you.

Hiyo is a non-profit social app made in the Netherlands by Robert Monther. Contact: Hiyo's own email address is added here before the public launch. Until then, use Report in the app, or message the Hiyo team account in the app.
