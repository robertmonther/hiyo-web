Terms of use

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Test version. These rules are in force for Hiyo's test. They will be checked and completed before the public launch; if anything changes, the app asks you to read and accept them again.

1. Who runs Hiyo

Hiyo is a free, non-profit social app made in the Netherlands by Robert Monther ("Hiyo", "we"). These terms and Hiyo's constitution are the agreement between you and us.

2. Accepting the terms

You accept these terms and the constitution in the app before you can post, comment or send messages. If the terms change, the app asks you to read and accept them again.

3. Who can join

  • You must be at least 13. Under 18 you get a safer Hiyo: no 18+ posts or groups, and chats only with friends.
  • One account per person, with true information about your age. You keep your password safe.
  • No accounts for media outlets, social networks, political parties, states, armies or armed groups (constitution, article 2).

4. Your content

What you post stays yours. You allow Hiyo to store it, show it to the people you chose (everyone, friends, friends of friends, a group) and process it in the ways you ask for (for example cutting reels from your long video). This permission ends when you delete the content or your account. Only post what you have the right to share: your own photos, videos and music, or work you have permission to use.

5. Not allowed on Hiyo

  • Any sexual content or behaviour involving anyone under 18, including grooming and sextortion (zero tolerance, see child safety).
  • Promoting war, selling or advertising weapons, raising money for weapons or armed groups, terrorism and violent extremism.
  • Threats, violence, hate against people for who they are, harassment, bullying and sharing someone's private details.
  • Intimate images of anyone without their consent; selling sex.
  • Adult content without the 18+ label.
  • Illegal goods and services, scams, spam, fake accounts, impersonation, and malware.
  • Content that breaks the law of the Netherlands or the European Union, or someone else's rights.

6. Reporting, blocking and moderation

Every post, reel, profile, group and message has Report, and you can block anyone: they can't see you or message you any more. We review reports and remove content that breaks these terms; clear cases are hidden automatically while we look. We can limit, suspend or close accounts that break the rules, especially for serious or repeated cases, and we report child sexual abuse material and threats to life to the authorities. If we remove your content or limit your account, we tell you why, and you can ask us to look again.

7. Bub, Hiyo's AI

Bub helps you post, cut videos into reels and tune your feed. It acts only when you confirm, and it can make mistakes, so check what it prepares. What you send Bub is processed by the AI services named in the privacy policy.

8. A free service in its test phase

Hiyo is free and has no outside ads. During the test, features change and the app may sometimes be unavailable. We do our best to keep your content safe, but please keep your own copies of what matters to you.

9. Leaving Hiyo

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account (see how). We may close an account that seriously or repeatedly breaks these terms.

10. Responsibility

You are responsible for what you post and send. Hiyo is responsible as far as Dutch law requires; nothing in these terms limits your rights as a consumer.

11. Law and illegal content

Dutch law applies. To tell us about illegal content (as the EU Digital Services Act allows), use Report in the app or the contact below; we confirm, decide and tell you what we did.
