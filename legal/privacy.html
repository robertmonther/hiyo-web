Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Test version. These rules are in force for Hiyo's test. They will be checked and completed before the public launch; if anything changes, the app asks you to read and accept them again.

Hiyo is built on one promise: your data is yours. We keep only what Hiyo needs to work, we never sell it, and we never share it with advertisers or other apps.

1. Who is responsible

Robert Monther, who makes Hiyo in the Netherlands, is the controller of your personal data.

2. What Hiyo stores

  • Your account: email address, password (stored scrambled by our login provider, never readable), username, display name, photo, and what you add to your profile (bio, city, interests).
  • Your birth date: only to keep people under 18 safe and to unlock 18+ content for adults. Only you see it.
  • What you create: posts, photos, videos, reels, comments, likes, saves, shares, groups and messages, including voice messages and files you send. A location is stored only when you choose to send it in a chat.
  • Your connections: friend requests and friends, follows, blocks, and reports you make.
  • Your settings: language, theme, privacy, notification and feed settings, and which version of these rules you accepted.
  • Notifications: if you turn on push, the address your phone or browser gives us to deliver them.
  • Online status: when you were last active, only if you allow "show when I'm online".
  • Bub: what you ask Bub and the photos, voice messages and files you send it, to answer you; and a daily count of your requests, to keep within the free limits.
  • Technical: your IP address is used for a short time to stop mass sign-ups and attacks, and appears in our providers' server logs, which they delete after a short time.

Hiyo does not collect advertising IDs, your contacts, your browsing history or your location in the background, and it has no ad or tracking tools. On the web, Hiyo keeps your login and a copy of what you last saw on your own device, so the app opens fast; logging out removes it.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • To run Hiyo for you: your account, your posts and messages, your feed (to perform our agreement with you).
  • To keep Hiyo safe: age protection, reports, blocking and moderation (our legitimate interest and legal obligations).
  • Push notifications and showing that you're online: only with your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings.

4. Who helps us run Hiyo

These providers process data for us, only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: database, file storage, login and servers, in the European Union (Paris).
  • GitHub (Microsoft): hosts the web app's files and runs our job that cuts long videos into reels.
  • Groq: the AI behind Bub (text, understanding photos, turning voice into text), in the United States.
  • Google (Gemini API) and Hugging Face: backup AI services for Bub when Groq is busy.
  • Pollinations: makes an image from your description when you ask Bub for one (only the description is sent).
  • The push service of your phone or browser (for example Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft), to deliver notifications.

Some of these providers are outside the European Union. We use them under the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

5. Who sees your content

The people you choose: everyone, friends, friends of friends or a group. Photos and videos of posts that are not for everyone are private files that open only through links that expire, made only for people allowed to see the post. Messages are visible to the members of the chat. Messages are encrypted on their way and in storage, but are not yet end-to-end encrypted; that is on our plan.

6. How long we keep it

Until you delete it or your account. A message you delete for everyone is wiped. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, comments, messages and files are deleted. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short time before they are overwritten.

7. Your rights

You can see and download your data (Settings → Download my data), correct it, delete it (Settings → Delete account), object to or limit how we use it, and take it with you. Write to us to use any of these rights. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

8. Children

Hiyo is for people from 13. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we delete it.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted, every table in our database has rules that let each person read only what they may see, and private files open only through expiring links. If something goes wrong with your data, we tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Changes

When this policy changes, the app asks you to read and accept it again.
