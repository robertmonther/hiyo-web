Hiyo's constitution

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Test version. These rules are in force for Hiyo's test. They will be checked and completed before the public launch; if anything changes, the app asks you to read and accept them again.

Hiyo is a movement of thought, not a party: a home for the silent majority who want a planet without weapons, without wars and without extreme gaps between rich and poor. We don't need the whole world. A hundred thousand people who share this way of thinking is enough.

1. No war

Hiyo has one political view: the planet should have no wars. Posts, reels, groups, profiles and messages that promote or glorify war, sell or advertise weapons, or raise money for weapons, armies or armed groups are removed. Talking about how to end wars, how to heal from them and how to live better is welcome.

2. People, not institutions

You join as a person. News and media outlets, existing social networks, political parties, states, armies and armed groups can't have accounts on Hiyo, and no one may use Hiyo to promote those that pursue war.

3. A free life

Love, romance, friendship, work, money, art, sexuality, and who you are and whom you love are free to talk about here. Adult content (18+) must carry the 18+ label: it stays hidden from people under 18 and covered until an adult chooses to see it.

4. Your feed is yours

Hiyo shows what you ask for, through your feed settings or by asking Bub, Hiyo's AI. Nothing changes your feed without your command, and you can always see and change the settings yourself.

5. Your data is yours

No ads from outside companies, no tracking cookies, and nothing sold or shared with other apps or networks. You can download your data or delete it, with your account, at any time.

6. Respect and safety

No hate, threats, harassment or bullying. Nothing sexual involving anyone under 18, ever: it is removed, the account is closed and it is reported to the authorities. People from 13 years old may join; under 18 they get a safer Hiyo.

7. Support, never begging

People here can support each other's projects and real needs. Support is offered freely, never pressed for.

8. Bub follows this constitution too

Bub, Hiyo's AI, works only on your request and follows these articles. If you ask Bub for something against them, it says no and can explain why.

Hiyo is a non-profit social app made in the Netherlands by Robert Monther. Contact: Hiyo's own email address is added here before the public launch. Until then, use Report in the app, or message the Hiyo team account in the app.
