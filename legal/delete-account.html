Delete your Hiyo account

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Test version. These rules are in force for Hiyo's test. They will be checked and completed before the public launch; if anything changes, the app asks you to read and accept them again.

You can delete your Hiyo account and everything in it at any time. Deleting is permanent and can't be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Hiyo and go to your Profile, then the gear (Settings).
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The same works on the web: log in at Hiyo on the web, open Settings and choose Delete account.

Without the app

If you can't open the app, send a request from the email address of your account (see the contact below) with the subject "Delete my Hiyo account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photo, posts, reels, comments, likes, saves, friends and follows.
  • Your messages, including those you sent in groups, and every photo, video, voice message and file you uploaded.
  • Your settings, notifications, birth date and what you asked Bub.

Reports you made stay, without your name, so we can finish handling them. Copies may stay in our providers' backups for a short time before they are overwritten. We can't remove copies other people made, such as screenshots.

Delete only some data

You can delete a single post from its menu, delete a message for everyone, and download all your data first in Settings → Download my data.
